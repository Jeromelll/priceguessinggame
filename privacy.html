Privacy

Updated 12 October 2026.

Optional sign-in

You can play without an account, or sign in with Google, GitHub or X. We store your provider account identifier, display name, profile image URL and verified email when available. We do not store your provider password or access token. Security cookies protect sign-in, and a session cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. Sign out to remove the session. Different providers remain separate accounts.

Scores and local storage

Submitted scores and player names appear on the leaderboard. Signing in attaches new scores to your provider identity; your existing Google scores retain their original identity. Anonymous play uses a random identifier stored on your device. Your streak and current game progress are stored in your browser.

Hosting and analytics

Cloudflare hosts and protects the game and processes technical request information under its privacy policy. Feature analytics record aggregate page and interaction events without account identifiers, email addresses or submitted bid amounts.

Your requests

For access, correction or deletion requests, contact the publisher at ads@be-winner.com. We may ask you to verify the account concerned. We do not sell your personal information.
